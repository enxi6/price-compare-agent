- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Status:** LIST ONLY — candidate list for user approval. Nothing in this file has been added to the catalog. No repo file other than this one was modified; nothing committed or pushed.
- **How it was built:** 6 research groups (Sony/Bose/JBL; Logitech/Razer/HyperX/Corsair; Apple/Samsung/Nintendo/Garmin/Keychron/Microsoft/Lenovo; Anker/Alienware/Dell/HP/Google/Amazon/Meta/ASUS; follow-up audio/gaming peripherals; follow-up wearables/monitors/tablets/accessories) → 56 proposable candidates. Synthesis trimmed 6 to fit exactly 50 (see "Cut at synthesis") and held 3 as Reserve (see bottom). Starting material was the 14 firm passes + 5 alternates cut at batch-3 synthesis, re-verified this round — several brief-supplied identifiers turned out wrong (see "Corrections to brief-supplied identifiers") and three brief items do not exist as specified (JBL Live Beam 2, Sony INZONE H5 II, Sony BRAVIA Theatre Bar 1000).
- **Rules applied:** DECISIONS 001–005, 015, 016, 020, 021, 027, 057, 068. Mainstream/new products from brands already in the catalog; US-market versions only; seller-direct only (brand direct or retailer direct); no Amazon in any form, no third-party/marketplace sellers, no parallel imports, no international variants, no second-hand/refurbished/clearance-only/discontinued stock. Every candidate checked against all 350 catalog rows (key/model/MPN/UPC, including different colors/configs of the same model) — no duplicates (verified again at synthesis by script: 0 collisions).
- **Identifier honesty:** UPC/MPN is marked VERIFIED only when found on a retailer/manufacturer spec page or an official source. Otherwise PENDING, with the lead value (if any) and what was checked. Amazon-only values are never treated as verified. Keychron EAN + SKU follows the K8 Ultra 8K precedent (identifiers recorded as EAN + SKU, not UPC). Suggested `key` values follow existing catalog slug conventions and are proposals only.
- **Identifier tally for the 50:** UPC/EAN VERIFIED 23 / PENDING 27. The verified rate (46%) is higher than batch 3's draft (36%) and batch 3's as-added rate (30%) — the follow-up groups and the re-verification of the batch-3 held passes delivered most of the verified codes (Logitech 7, Sony/Bose/JBL 3, Anker/ASUS 4, Garmin/Samsung 3, audio follow-up 4, wearables follow-up 2). No rule change is proposed; identifiers stay pending per the standing rule. The tally here is counted from the per-entry markings in this file.
- **New categories in this batch:** none. Streaming-devices gains a second model (Fire TV Stick 4K Max); tablets gain one (iPad mini A17 Pro); power-banks/accessories gain one (MagGo). No new category needs approval.
- **Brands with zero models in the final 50:** Bose, Microsoft, Lenovo, Alienware, Meta. Bose yielded no passing model this round (Revolve+ II is clearance/EOL-only; Ultra Open 1st gen is clearance/superseded; 2nd gen is already in the catalog).

Method & principles

How this site compares prices, what it can and cannot verify, and where its money does and does not come from. No black-box scores.

1. Ranking: delivered total, nothing else

Offers in a verified ranking are ordered by delivered total: item price + shipping + estimated tax − discounts. Offers with unknown shipping rank behind offers with known shipping, because an unknown total is a lower bound, not a delivered price. Ties break by delivery days, then seller rating. There is no composite score and no "recommended for you".

2. Same product means matching identifier

Only a matching UPC, EAN, MPN or official SKU counts as the same product. Similar titles go to the "suspected same product" appendix and never enter the main ranking. An identifier is recorded only when two independent sources state it for the exact variant (color, storage and connectivity included). Identifiers are never invented, never carried across variants, revisions or regions.

Current state, stated plainly: As of 2026-11-07, 84 offers across 76 models are identifier-matched and appear in verified rankings. The remaining offers are title-matched only. New identifier-returning sources are added source by source; a model enters the verified ranking only when a quote's identifier matches its verified identifier.

3. Data sources, in tiers

Official APIs and feeds first, then affiliate catalogs, then structured data, then read-only browser verification. A channel that is blocked or prohibited by its terms of service stops there: no bypassing verification, no fake identity, no rotating proxies.

Identifier-bearing quotes currently come from Shopify public product JSON (tier 1), browser-verified retailer spec pages (tier 4).

Amazon is not covered in v1. Its access wall is treated as a permanent property of the retailer, not a temporary failure. Only legitimate channels will ever be used (its official API, or licensed data providers); none is live yet, and no Amazon spend is approved.

4. Which quotes count

Only seller-direct or retailer-direct quotes: the merchant itself selling the product. Marketplace and third-party seller rows are excluded, even when cheaper. Only US-market goods (US MPN/UPC, sold by US retailers). Only new units: used, refurbished and open-box are out of scope for Phase 1. Bundles are excluded at snapshot time.

5. Freshness

Every price carries its merchant, source link and fetch time. Prices are as of their snapshot date, shown on every page. Anything older than 24 hours is labeled "Needs re-check" and is never presented as a live price.

6. Money and disclosure

This site currently earns no commission from any quote shown. If affiliate links are added later, they will be disclosed on this page and will never affect ranking. There are no ads and no sponsored placements. Ranking factors contain buyer interests only: total price, delivery, reputation.

7. Privacy

This is a static site. No accounts, no login, no tracking, no data collected. Search runs in your browser; nothing you type leaves the page.
